Heart of the City

Aurakatu 12, Turku

We are conveniently situated directly in downtown Turku on the historical Aurakatu street, only moments away from the Market Square (Kauppatori). Our central address makes it effortless to visit during your work lunch break or post-office commute.

Step into a spacious space designed with tall custom windows, premium acoustic finishes, and comfortable warm lighting.

By Public Transit

Almost all Föli metropolitan bus lines halt at Turku Market Square, less than 200 meters away from our shop entrance door.

Parking Details

Paid street-level parking slots are widely available directly across Aurakatu. Alternatively, utilize the nearby Toriparkki underground structure.

Walking Landmarks

Look out for the elegant heritage architecture. We are placed closely near the historic Aura riverfront walk corridor.
